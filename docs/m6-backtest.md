# M6 backtest: stopped-car inference

Generated by `npm run backtest` on 2026-11-15 from 32 ingested sessions.

Each yellow or double-yellow flag incident (a group of neighbouring sectors raised together) is evaluated one minute after it was raised, with the thresholds in `lib/incident/stopped.ts`.

Ground truth comes from data the detector does not use: race-control messages naming cars in an incident whose bracketed time is within 90 s before to 30 s after the flag, and cars that stopped for good and are classified as not finishing.

- **right**: a detected car is named by race control or retired there
- **wrong**: race control named other cars, none detected
- **unconfirmed**: a car was detected but nothing names it (a spin without a stewards' note is common)
- **missed**: race control named cars but nothing was detected
- **empty**: nothing detected and nothing named (debris, marshals, a car off line)
- **in sector**: for circuits with MultiViewer markers, whether the first car to stop was inside the flagged stretch (cars stopped elsewhere are listed apart in the dashboard and not scored)
